Investigate the Zariski cancellation problem over $\mathbb{C}$ in dimension $3$.

Seek a finitely generated $\mathbb{C}$-domain

$$
A=\mathbb{C}[x_1,\ldots,x_m]/I,
$$

where $I$ is prime and $\dim A=3$, such that

$$
A[u]\cong_{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2,x_3,u],
$$

but

$$
A\not\cong_{\mathbb{C}}\mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2,x_3].
$$

Start with smooth hypersurfaces

$$
A=\mathbb{C}[x,y,z,t]/(f),
$$

and examine known candidates such as Russell and Koras–Russell threefolds. Use invariants such as the Makar–Limanov invariant, Derksen invariant, class group, units, locally nilpotent derivations, and topology to prove that $A\not\cong\mathbb{C}^{[3]}$.

For any proposed candidate, either construct and verify an explicit isomorphism

$$
A[u]\cong\mathbb{C}^{[4]},
$$

or explain precisely why this remains unproved. Clearly distinguish established results, open problems, and speculation. Do not claim a counterexample without complete proofs of both required statements.
